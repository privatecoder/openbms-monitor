---
title: Health report
short: Evaluates the recording over weeks: cell spread at rest, cycles, warmest cell per pack and the current split within each pair.
related: topic.history, topic.recording, topic.pairs, topic.cellAnalysis
---
- **Where:** History → Report, for the installation chosen in the sidebar, also without a connection. Period 7, 30 or 90 days up to now or everything; counted in calendar days of this PC's local time. Read only, nothing is written.
- **Rest spread** (highest minus lowest cell) is the comparable number: under load the voltages sag differently and the spread grows without any cell ageing. At rest means: current between −2 and +2 A, for single values also in the poll before (a cell still recovers for a few seconds after load), for minute values throughout the minute; then the minute's largest spread counts. A minute's largest spread is a different measure than single polls: a day with single values uses only those, a day with only minute values their maxima, never mixed. Shown are median and 95th percentile of the last day and the trend over the period. A pack is marked when its value is above 1.5 times the median of the other packs (with two packs: the partner's) and at least 10 mV above it; compared on the latest day on which at least two packs have 30 rest values each of the same kind (single or minute values), and only among those packs. If there is no such day or a pack is missing on it, the hint says “not rated” with the reason in the tooltip, not “unremarkable”. It is also marked when the value rises by 10 mV or more in the period; the trend needs at least 3 days with 180 rest values each (single values) or 30 (minute values) and takes only days of one kind, never across the change. In the daily chart minute values have their own dotted curves; the CSV gives the kind in the column spread_raw (1 = single values). Next to it is the spread over all values (95th percentile). Which cell causes a flag is under “Cells”; the hints explain themselves in their tooltip.
- **Spread only from a date:** minute values of earlier versions did not store the minute's spread and current range; they are missing from these figures. The report says from when they exist. Cycles and temperature count for them too.
- **Cycles:** the last count of each day and from it the cycles per 30 days. If the counter drops (e.g. after “Clear history and counters”), a new segment begins; the report computes no rate across such a jump. The counter counts whole cycles only; the rate therefore appears from 7 days of counts (before that “–”). A pack is marked when it counts more than 25 % and at least 2 whole cycles faster than the median of the other packs, and only when that median gained at least 8 cycles in the period, all packs were recorded for about the same time and no counter was cleared. Then it carries more than the others or its counter runs differently (capacity, firmware).
- **Warmest cell:** the highest value in the period; marked from 5 K above the median of the other packs, compared only over the days on which all packs have temperatures and at least half the bank's median row count (otherwise “not rated”); the flag names the value compared.
- **Pairs:** for the pairs set under Overview → Pairs. For each single value of the plus pack the report takes the nearest one of the minus pack, at most 3 s away, and counts it only when both currents flow the same way and the pair carries at least 40 A together (20 A per pack as in the live comparison); against each other the packs equalize, they do not share a load. Shown is the plus pack's mean share. If on a day it is more than 5 points away from 50 % over at least 30 minutes with samples (a minute counts with one sample in it, not as a minute observed throughout), the day is marked; the row names the pack carrying more. All samples count. How many of them fit a difference in state of charge (discharging, the emptier pack gives less; charging, the fuller pack takes less; from 3 points SOC difference) is given as a possible explanation in the tooltip and the CSV (column equalizing), as the live comparison shows it beside the warning. Single values on the full minute count only with a value of the same pack less than a minute away: old imported minute values are stored as one value on the minute, their time rounded.
- **Pair figures from single values only:** minute values average the current and are no good for the comparison. Pair figures therefore exist only for the days in which the recording still keeps every poll (default 30 days, set under Recording). The pair's output voltage is not in the report: the recording holds the pack voltage, not the voltage at the output the live comparison uses.
- **Course and export:** clicking a row shows its daily values as a chart. “Save as CSV” writes all daily values (one row per pack and day with the BMS balancing bits, per pack the cycle rate, per pack the cell analysis with one row per cell, one row per pair and day), dates in local time.
- **Cells:** per pack the cells the cell analysis does not find matching, with their assessment and the reason (details under cell analysis and in the pack details).

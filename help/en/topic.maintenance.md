---
title: Maintenance on the pack bus
short: Makes the pack bus of a bank quiet so that its packs can be flashed, and gives it back to the master afterwards.
related: topic.firmware, topic.buses
---
- **Why the master interferes:** a master with slaves (DIP 5–8 not all OFF) polls RS485-1/2 every second and ignores the restart command meanwhile. While it polls the bus is not quiet and no pack can be flashed. It stops when **DIP 5–8 of the master are OFF** (any firmware, it reads the switches continuously) or when a maintenance build has its **marker** set. A single pack has no slaves; its bus is quiet anyway.
- **One screen:** on top the status line, refreshed every 2 s: “Bus quiet” (green), “Master polls N slaves” (amber) or “not connected”. With a CAN socket set up, the line below shows what the master reports. While the bus is not quiet, the app shows “Make the bus quiet”. Once it is quiet, it asks addresses 0–15 once with 0x51 and lists every pack that answers with its version and “Check & flash”; “Search again” repeats that; “Cancel” ends a running search before the next address and lists what answered until then. Above the list you choose the image once. After an update, or while the marker is on, “Done” appears at the bottom: “Release master” for a maintenance build with the marker on, otherwise the reminder to set DIP 5–8 of the master back. While an update runs, the app sends nothing else on the pack bus.
- **A – single pack:** connect RS485-1/2; the bus is quiet, the pack is listed, flash it.
- **B – bank with an original master:** set DIP 5–8 of the master OFF (note the old positions). The status line turns green by itself; then flash any pack, the master too. Afterwards set DIP 5–8 back; the status line shows “Master polls N slaves” again.
- **C – bank with a maintenance build on the master:** set up the CAN socket too and press “Halt master”: the app sets the marker over the CAN socket (command 0x64 to address 0) and waits until the master reports 0 slaves and the bus is quiet; then flash any pack. Afterwards “Release master” (marker off). The marker is stored in flash: it survives restarts, power loss and an update of the master with a maintenance build. If the installation knows the master's battery serial number (String 1), the app switches the marker only when the master at the CAN socket reports exactly that one; another (“another master”) or none readable (“Master serial number not readable at the CAN socket: bank not confirmed”) prevents it.
- **C′ – maintenance build on the master, the new master image is not a maintenance build** (original or custom): the original firmware does not know the marker; it would poll right after the update, and the marker word would stay hidden in flash. Therefore: set DIP 5–8 of the master OFF, “Release master” (while the maintenance build still runs), flash the master, set DIP 5–8 back. The app shows this order only in this case at the master's row, and “Check & flash” stays locked there until the marker reads off and the bus is quiet. The order matters: releasing first and setting the DIP switches afterwards lets the master poll again meanwhile; no harm, you just wait until the bus is quiet again. If the master cannot be read right now and its marker was last seen on, the app refuses the master's update too.
- **What the master reports (CAN socket):** a maintenance build 1.1 reports version, build ID, the raw marker word and the slave count it works with (“16.06.13 MAINT 1.1”). 1.0 reports only marker and slave count (“MAINT 1.0 compatible”). The original firmware does not know the command (“0x64 not supported”; this does not prove which image runs). A marker word other than “off” and “on” reads “marker sector with unknown content”: then the app offers no “Release master” (the build refuses it anyway). A maintenance build with an unknown build ID is only shown; the app offers neither halt nor release for it. The app sends “Release master” only on the button and only while the master reports the marker on; never by itself.
- **Inverter:** while the master does not poll it reports only its own pack to the inverter (lower charge and discharge limits). While the master itself is flashed, CAN stops entirely. The app does not control the inverter; it only points this out.
- **Marker on:** while the last read shows the marker on, every view shows “Maintenance mode active: the master polls no slaves” with “Release master” on top, also after an app restart; the line goes when a read shows the marker off.
- **Pack may hang in its bootloader:** if an update breaks off after the restart, the pack may have no valid application left; the bootloader stays. The app remembers this per RS485-1/2 port, also across a restart, and shows “Pack X: update interrupted, the pack may hang in its bootloader” on top. Because bootloader frames carry no address, a silent pack in its bootloader would be written along with the flash of another pack: while the note stands, the app therefore flashes no other pack, on no other port either (two names of the same gateway would be different ports to it). Reading, DIP and marker actions stay free. The note goes once pack X answers 0x51 again with the same BMS serial number (String 2) as before the update (pack search or “Check pack”), or when you confirm “Pack X is disconnected or repaired”. If the serial number was not readable before the update (empty, placeholder), it stays until that confirmation. Flashing a pack that hangs in its bootloader is not possible in the app yet: the updater needs an answer of the application first.
- **Right installation:** with an installation chosen, the app reads the master's BMS serial number (0xA4 at address 0 on the pack bus) before every update and stores it the first time without asking. If the master later reports another one, the app refuses the update (“Another installation hangs on this port”) and offers “This installation has a new master: take over the serial number”. If nobody answers 0xA4 (e.g. a pack without this command), the flash goes ahead and “Installation not checked” is shown. The app also reads String 2 of the target pack: if it is another board than the one the installation knows at this address, the update is refused (“Another board … answers at the target”); without a known board or a readable serial number it goes ahead as before. In the “One pack” tab on another port than the installation's, the app checks neither the master nor the installation.
- **Flashing belongs to an installation:** the panel shows the installation chosen in the sidebar; with “View only” nothing is flashed. Every update log carries the installation's id in its name and is listed under **Installations** at its pack, and so is every line of `maint-events.log`.
- **Ports:** the panel only shows the installation's CAN socket and RS485-1/2; they are changed under **Installations** (“Change under Installations”). Without an RS485-1/2 port it shows only “No RS485-1/2 port for A: set it up under Installations”.
- **RS485-1/2 over a network gateway:** released for testing, untested. The gateway channel must be set to 19200 baud; before an update over the gateway the app says “Update over a network gateway: untested”. A monitor on the same gateway is disconnected first, as on the same USB port. If the network link breaks after the erase, the pack may stay in its bootloader (see above); the app repeats nothing.

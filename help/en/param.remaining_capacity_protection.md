---
title: SOC protection below
short: Below this SOC the discharge MOSFET opens; charging stays allowed. While discharging, the SOC holds 1 % above it until a cell reaches the low-voltage warning.
related: topic.params
---
Message: event 6 bit 3 (enable: function switch 5, bit 3). The comparison uses the SOC rounded to whole percent. Charge activation briefly lifts the protection at intervals.

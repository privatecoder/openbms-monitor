---
title: Messages
short: Warnings, protections, faults and notices the BMS currently reports.
related: status.mosfets
---
- **Warning:** reported only. If all packs have a charge-related warning, the master lowers the charge limit to 10 A in total.
- **Protection:** the BMS opens a MOSFET (charging or discharging blocked).
- **Fault:** hardware or calibration problem.
- **Info:** a state, e.g. heating requested or waiting for recharge.

Every message has its own info icon with cause and effect.

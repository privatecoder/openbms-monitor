---
title: Lock after events
short: After this many transient over-current or short-circuit events without a pause, the discharge MOSFET stays off until the pack detects charging. Only with function switch 4, bit 6 or 7.
related: topic.params
---
Events count when there is no 301 s pause between them. The lock reports event 5 bit 6 (transient over-current) or bit 7 (short circuit). Normal charge and discharge over-current protection does not count.

---
title: Hard stop
short: A property of the firmware image: a full pack waiting for intermittent recharge opens its own charge MOSFET. The image reports it itself (format 3), otherwise the app concludes it from behaviour.
related: topic.firmwareProfile, switch.5.1
---
- **Without hard stop (original):** while a full pack with intermittent recharge (function switch 5, bit 1) waits for its SOC to fall, only the master lowers the charge limit once all packs are that far. The pack's charge MOSFET stays closed.
- **With hard stop:** the full, waiting pack opens its own charge MOSFET and takes no more charge current, even if the inverter ignores the charge limit.
- **Settable from MAINT 1.6.3:** there no fixed build sets the hard stop but function switch 0, bits 6/7: bit 7 = 1 takes the build's default (off in the published image), else bit 6 applies. Under Parameters “Hard stop at a recharge pause” switches it on or off with one switch; the whole parameter block is written with only this change, both bits together, every other bit as read. The pack view shows “set under Parameters (default …)”. Editing parameters, a file or an earlier state never change bits 6/7. With MAINT 1.5.2 the hard stop is fixed in the image.
- **What the display says:** “on” or “off” is reported by the image itself (identity format 3); “unknown” means the firmware does not report it (format 2). It is a property of the flashed image, independent of the maintenance state, the release bits and whether the charge MOSFET is open or closed right now.
- **Observed:** where the firmware does not report it (format 2, original firmware, slaves), the app goes by the pack's behaviour: waiting for recharge with its charge MOSFET off and no other blocking cause in the status reads “on (observed …)”, otherwise “unknown”. This is a conclusion from behaviour, not proof from the image. Excluded are the blocks visible in the status (cell and pack over-voltage protection, charge over- and under-temperature protection, ambient and power stage protection, charge over-current protection, charger over-voltage, “cell too low to charge”, reverse polarity, output connection fault, faulty charge MOSFET), the current limiter, the heater and a pack switched off. Some uncertainty remains because some blocks are not visible in the status: the standby shutdown that blocks charging 60 s before switching off, and the raw cell temperature charge protection while discharging (from 16.06.11; the status shows the temperature message by current direction). A charge MOSFET on while waiting disproves nothing (the firmware switches it back on after 10 s of its own discharge over 10 A). A flash of the pack by the app ends the observation; a flash outside the app is not seen. Via the CAN socket the app stores it only when the master reports this installation's battery serial number; with original firmware there is no per-pack serial there, so a slave board swapped at an address goes unnoticed. Version numbers do not count.

---
title: Switch state
short: Which power switches of the pack are currently on.
related: topic.alarms
---
- **Discharge/charge MOSFET:** off while a protection is active.
- **Current limiter:** hardware stage used for charging after charge over-current (or optionally from 10 A).
- **Heater:** only when heating is requested and the pack is charging or a charger is detected.

---
title: Current share
short: A pack's current divided by the mean current of all packs in the same time slice.
related: topic.history, topic.pairs
---
- **1.00** means the pack carries exactly its share. 0.5 means half as much, 1.2 means 20 % more.
- Shown only where the mean current per pack is at least 5 A and at least three quarters of the packs answered in that slice (the mean refers to those). Below that, equalizing currents between the packs dominate and the share means nothing.
- A lastingly low share points to a higher resistance in that pack's current path (plug, cable, fuse, switch); a pack with a higher SOC, on the other hand, gives more for a while when discharging.

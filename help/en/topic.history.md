---
title: History
short: Shows recordings as charts over time.
related: topic.recording, value.share
---
- **Open:** the list shows the recordings in the app's folder, newest first. Files from elsewhere (or a copy from another computer) are opened with **Open file**.
- **Charts per pack:** current, current share, SOC, pack voltage, highest and lowest cell, cell difference, warmest and coldest cell. Each pack keeps its colour everywhere; the pack buttons hide single packs.
- **Master** (only if the recording contains the system values): total current with charge and discharge limit, SOC, voltage with charge voltage, the bank's cell extremes.
- **Cells of one pack:** all cell voltages of a chosen pack, e.g. to see balancing or a weak cell.
- **Zoom:** drag across a chart; all charts follow. Double-click or **Whole recording** resets. The cursor shows the same moment in every chart, the values are listed below each chart.
- **Resolution:** each chart shows at most about 1500 points. Each point is the mean of the answers in its time slice; for highest and lowest cell, cell difference and temperatures the extreme, so peaks do not disappear. Zooming in makes it finer, down to every single poll.
- **Gaps:** a briefly missing answer (up to about three polls) is bridged; longer outages stay visible as a gap.

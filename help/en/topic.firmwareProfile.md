---
title: Firmware profile of the connection
short: After connecting via the CAN socket the app asks the master for its firmware and sets itself up by it.
related: topic.patchGates, topic.hardStop, topic.maintenance, topic.buses
---
- **When:** on every connect via the CAN socket, on exactly that port, before polling starts (command 0x64 to address 0). The profile holds only for this connection; another port, a new connect or an update is checked again. Via RS485-1/2 there is no master profile (the master does not answer there while it polls); the app reads a pack's identity there at its own address in the firmware view's pack list.
- **Patched firmware (from EMU1101-EXT Light 1.5.4 / Full 1.6.4):** the line shows only the version (listed: EMU1101-EXT Light 1.5.4, EMU1101-EXT Light 1.5.5, EMU1101-EXT Full 1.6.4, EMU1101-EXT Full 1.6.5, 16.06.13 MAINT 1.6; otherwise the reported version such as “EMU1101-EXT Full 1.6.6”, with “build not listed” in the tooltip; an unknown revision as “16.06.?”). Maintenance state, releases and hard stop apply per pack and show in the maintenance window, the parameter table and the pack view. The app then reads every pack's serial numbers via the CAN socket when connecting, offers “Halt master” and sets up the Parameters view by the release bits. Whether the app supports patched firmware follows from its identity and version (from Light 1.5.4 / Full 1.6.4), not from the list; the list only gives names. Which functions it offers follows from the image flags and read capabilities the pack reports itself, the releases from the freshly read bits of function switch 0.
- **Unknown patched firmware:** an older or other patch level. The app then uses original functions only and sends no patch commands.
- **0x64 not supported:** typical of original firmware, but no proof of it. All original functions work as usual.
- **Firmware not recognised:** no answer or no valid one. “Check again” asks once more.
- **Slaves:** newer builds answer the identity (64/03) at every pack's own address too. The app asks each found pack once per connection in the background (at most 1.5 s per pack, up to 3 s over a network gateway with slow answers; “not checked yet” until then) and shows version, build and hard stop in its pack view, never in the master profile. A valid identity from EMU1101-EXT Light 1.5.4 / Full 1.6.4 proves the pack for this connection (“live”, not retractable); another valid identity (older patch, other format) sets a stored proof aside for this connection. No answer or an error answer says nothing: older slaves do not answer, and a stored proof then still holds. If a pack stayed silent, the app asks again on the next parameter read via the CAN socket. Maintenance state, halt and release exist only at master 0. The app reads serial numbers via the CAN socket per pack by that pack's own proof; “read via CAN socket” works by hand at any time. Residual case: a flash outside the app to original firmware with an unchanged BMS serial gives no identity, and an old stored proof then still holds.

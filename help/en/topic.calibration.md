---
title: Calibration and CAN protocol
short: Adjust a pack's current zero point, current and voltage and set the CAN protocol to the inverter, as with BatteryMonitor. Only via RS485-1/2, under Parameters (maintenance functions), for the pack chosen at the BMS clock.
related: topic.params, alarm.ev8.b2, alarm.ev8.b3, alarm.ev8.b4
---
- **What a calibration does:** the BMS computes a new factor from its previous factor and the ratio reference/measured and stores it at once. All later readings of this pack shift with it: current, voltage, SOC counting and the protection thresholds. A wrong reference falsifies the pack until it is calibrated again with a good measurement; a new calibration corrects an old error as far as the BMS's acceptance limit reaches. The BMS does not let the factors be read.
- **Current zero point:** the momentary readings of both current channels become the zero point. **No current may flow**: open the pack's breaker or disconnect the pack cable and check with a clamp meter. The BMS shows a fixed 0 A in standby even when a little current flows, so its own value proves nothing, and the app checks nothing here. The BMS accepts the zero point while both channels are below 1 A: a current of up to almost 1 A left over stays as a **permanent offset** and falsifies current and SOC. Clears “Zero point not calibrated”.
- **Current:** steady current of **at least 10 A**, clamp meter around one pack cable of this pack, charging positive. Enter the measured value; the app shows the BMS current and the difference beside it. The BMS accepts deviations of up to 3 A only but checks no minimum current: a reference of 0.01 A against 2 A would divide the factor by 200, and because of the 3-A limit one calibration could not undo that. So the app enables the button only when the entered value and the BMS current are both at least 10 A and have the same direction. Clears “Current not calibrated”.
- **Voltage:** at rest (no current), MOSFETs on, multimeter at the **pack's output (P+/P−)**. The app divides the value by the number of cells and sends the mean cell voltage in mV (shown as “= 3.301 V per cell”). The BMS accepts it only when the cells are less than 20 mV apart, the cell mean is at most 30 mV from the reference and the output voltage at most 2 V from reference × cells. With the MOSFETs off the output is 0 V and the calibration is refused. It sets all cell factors and the pack voltage factor. Clears “Voltage not calibrated”.
- **Refused:** if a condition is not met, the BMS answers with a refusal (RTN 06); the app names the condition of that calibration. Nothing changes then.
- **After a calibration** the app reads the pack's values again and shows the new value and whether the “… not calibrated” message is gone. That is the value in RAM: the BMS does not check the write. Whether it was stored shows after a restart of the pack.
- **CAN protocol:** decides how the CAN socket talks to the inverter: 1 Pylontech/Deye/Luxpower/TBB, 2 Growatt SPF/SPH, 3 Victron, 4 SMA/Sofar, 5 Ginlong, 6 Studer. Only the master counts; the inverter must be set to the same protocol. The BMS stores the choice at once; the app then reads the name back from the pack.
- **Protocol 7 (MUST)** is not offered: it only really runs from firmware 16.06.11 on and switches CAN to 100 kbit/s. Older versions (06.04/06.05) store 7 but run protocol 1; after a later update the pack switches to MUST at start-up and another inverter loses the connection. The pack does not tell which of these versions runs (both report 16.06). A pack already on 7 is shown with its name.

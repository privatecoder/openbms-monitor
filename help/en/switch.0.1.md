---
title: Temperature sensor check
short: On: the BMS reports a sensor fault and disables heating for readings below −45 °C, at or above 100 °C (power stage: 130 °C), or a difference of at least 20 °C between the sensors it compares. Off: this check is skipped. Recommended: on. The only bit of this group the original firmware uses too.
related: topic.switches
---

---
title: Charging: under-temperature protection
short: Protection at a cell sensor: charging is blocked until the recovery value is reached.
related: topic.params
---
Message: event 3 bit 3 (enable: function switch 2, bit 3). The protection is reported only in the charging direction; from 16.06.11 the charge lock tests the raw protection bit regardless of the current direction.
LiFePO4 cells are usually rated for charging only from 0 °C; a lower value relies on the inverter and the charge limit.

---
title: Cell types
short: Assigns a cell model from the cell database to the packs, with the number of cells in parallel.
related: value.datasheet
---
- The assignment applies per installation (gateway or port), like the groups.
- **Cells in series** are read from the packs; you enter **parallel**. If the rated capacity in the BMS (parameter 58) is a multiple of the cell's, the app suggests the number.
- With a cell type every pack view shows the **limits per datasheet**, and the charge limit shows which charge current the cells allow at the current temperature.
- Own or changed cells are maintained under **Cells**.
